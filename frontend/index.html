Capabilities

Everything you need to
ship RL policies safely

From raw logged data to a statistically backed deployment decision — no A/B test required.

⚡

Multi-Estimator Analysis

IPS, WIS, DM, and DR estimators computed in parallel. Cross-validate estimates to catch bias before it reaches production.

📊

Bootstrap Confidence Intervals

95% CI via non-parametric bootstrap with configurable importance weight clipping to handle distribution shift.

🔒

Conservative Policy Gating

Deployment gate that only passes when the DR lower confidence bound clears a configurable safety margin above baseline.

🧸

Segment-wise Decisions

Per-segment CI analysis with partial rollout recommendations. Deploy to High-value first, gate Low-value until evidence accumulates.

💰

Business Simulator

Translate OPE metrics into revenue impact, risk scores, and plain-English deployment recommendations for non-technical stakeholders.

🔭

CQL Baseline Comparison

Overlay Conservative Q-Learning scores against DR estimates to validate the new policy beats a pessimistic offline baseline.

Workflow

Three steps from data
to deployment decision

01

Load Data & Policy

Point the dashboard at your logged behavior dataset (.npz) and the target policy checkpoint (.pth). No re-training needed.

02

Run OPE Estimators

Four estimators (IPS, WIS, DM, DR) run with configurable bootstrap CI and importance weight clipping. Results appear per-segment in seconds.

03

Get a Decision

The policy gate compares the DR lower bound against your safety margin. Get a PASS or HOLD with revenue impact and plain-English rationale.
